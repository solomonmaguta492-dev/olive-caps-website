How to order

Shop on the website and pay with M-Pesa, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you order.

  1. Choose a cap
    Pick the style, colour and quantity.
  2. Add to cart
    Keep shopping or go to your cart.
  3. Checkout
    Enter your name, phone and pickup or delivery details.
  4. Pay with M-Pesa
    Enter your PIN on the prompt sent to your phone.
  5. Get your caps
    We confirm your order and prepare it for pickup or delivery.

Paying with M-Pesa

After you place your order, tap Send M-Pesa prompt. A request appears on your phone; enter your PIN to pay.

Your order is confirmed only when M-Pesa confirms the payment. You'll see the M-Pesa reference on the order page.

Questions

How do I know my payment went through?

The order page changes to "Payment received" and shows your M-Pesa reference. You'll also get the usual M-Pesa SMS from Safaricom.

The M-Pesa prompt didn't appear. What do I do?

Check that your phone is on and unlocked, then tap "Send M-Pesa prompt" again. If it still fails, message us on WhatsApp with your order number.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, before we dispatch it. Contact us with your order number as soon as possible.

Do the caps fit everyone?

Most of our caps are adjustable and fit most adults. Each product lists its closure type in the description.

Can I return a cap?

If a cap arrives damaged or wrong, contact us within 3 days and we'll exchange it.
